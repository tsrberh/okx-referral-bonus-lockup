## What your friend has to do inside the 14-day window

The invitee needs advanced identity verification first, then whichever tasks appear on your referral page. Task types differ by user, which is why nobody can tell you in advance exactly what your friend will be asked to do.

Where a deposit task applies, it can be a one-time deposit or a cumulative net deposit, made by on-chain transfer or fiat deposit, completed within 14 days of registration. Deposits funded by internal transfer, red packets, reward payouts, or crypto bought through a P2P shared order are excluded from the calculation. That exclusion catches people who top up an account using balances they already hold on the platform and then wonder why the task never ticks over.

A trading task can be an on-time trade or a cumulative volume target, again inside the same 14-day window. Your friend is enrolled in the platform's New User Bonus Program automatically, and their own side of the reward also has to be claimed inside the current campaign period or it expires.

Read your own referral page before you promise a friend anything specific. The eligible channels, calculation methods, and completion criteria are all defined there, and they are not the same for every account.

## After the reward lands: lock-up, claim deadline, and the cap

Your reward arrives with a **7-day lock-up**. During those seven days it cannot be withdrawn or traded; it unlocks automatically when the period ends.

You then have to claim it manually, inside the campaign period, from the rewards area of the OKX app. Unclaimed rewards expire. They do not roll over into the next campaign cycle, and neither do the rewards from invitations you made after hitting the cap.

That cap matters more than it sounds. Each campaign cycle has a maximum reward ceiling, and invitations beyond it earn nothing at all. The ceiling resets when a new cycle begins, so a fresh campaign can reopen headroom you had already used up. Inviters can also unlock higher tiers as invitees complete additional or more advanced tasks, though OKX notes this may only apply to selected users.

Rewards are expected to be paid immediately after the task is completed, with system delays possible. If your friend finished the task and **48 hours** have passed with nothing in your account, contact customer service rather than waiting it out. And because rewards are paid in crypto, the value at the moment you can withdraw may differ from the value quoted when the task completed.

## Why a reward can still be locked after the 7 days are up

OKX runs rewards through risk systems with real-time monitoring. If nothing is flagged, the reward unlocks on schedule and becomes withdrawable or tradable. If something is flagged, it stays put while the account is reviewed. Fraudulent behaviour from an invited friend also gives the platform the right to revoke the reward outright.

## Six reasons the bonus never arrives

In rough order of how often each one bites:

**1. The code was added after registration.** Referral relationships can only be established at the moment of registration. Once registration or identity verification is complete, the relationship cannot be added or changed. If you signed up bare and later found a code, that account is not retroactively linkable.

**2. The friend's device was already bound to a different code.** If your friend clicked someone's referral link, or downloaded the app through a referral-coded route, at any point in the past **30 days**, that code may already be attached to their device. Browser cache makes it worse: scanning a new QR code or opening a different download link can still carry the old code through, and the registration field won't let them type a different one.

The official workaround is to have your friend register in a private or incognito browser window, check the registration form for a pre-filled code before submitting, confirm the correct code is entered, and only log into the app after registration and referral binding are complete.

**3. The invite crossed regions.** OKX gives an explicit example: a Brazilian user inviting a non-Brazilian user, or an APAC user inviting someone in the European Economic Area, leaves the inviter ineligible for the corresponding reward. Local rules can also mean certain referral features simply aren't offered in your market.

**4. The inviter used a sub-account.** Sub-accounts cannot take part in the referral program, receive rewards, or be invited as the invitee.

**5. The inviter is an affiliate.** Affiliate users earn through the affiliate program only and are not eligible for referral rewards from inviting friends. Upgrading to affiliate or sub-affiliate status ends referral reward eligibility.

**6. Risk controls fired.** Registering multiple accounts, using someone else's account or identity documents, supplying false KYC information, inflating trading data, wash trading, click farms and device farms, or violating local regulations all give OKX grounds to disqualify a participant, confiscate rewards, and suspend or freeze the account.

## FAQ

**Can I add or change a referral code after registering?**
No. The relationship is fixed at registration and cannot be modified once registration or identity verification is done. There is no support path that changes it afterward.

**Why can't I withdraw my reward after the 7-day lock-up ended?**
The reward is held by OKX's risk and monitoring systems. If no issue is found, it unlocks and becomes withdrawable or tradable. If activity on the account or on the invited friend's account is flagged, the reward can be withheld or revoked.

**My friend completed the task but nothing arrived after 48 hours. What now?**
Contact customer service. OKX states it will actively follow up on rewards not received within 48 hours of task completion.

**Can a sub-account join the referral program?**
No. Sub-accounts cannot participate, cannot receive rewards, and cannot be invited.

**Is the bonus the same for everyone?**
No. OKX states plainly that the reward structure depends on the campaign and on the tasks required, and the applicable structure is whatever is displayed on the referral page in your account.
